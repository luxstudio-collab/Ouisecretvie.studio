## My positions

*(öneri — confirm, change or delete. These are where the best reels come from.)*

1. A table is not furniture, it is a mood. The setting decides how a dinner
   feels before anyone sits down.
2. Beauty is in restraint: one color, one material, one strong idea beats a
   table crowded with everything.
3. The place matters as much as the table. Old walls, ruins and natural light
   do half the design.
4. AI is a sketchbook for ideas that would be impossible or very expensive to
   test in real life, not a replacement for real hosts and stylists.
